A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Does Stillterm lock my computer?+

Stillterm provides the animation. Your operating system controls locking, passwords, and authentication. Keep your normal security settings.

Can I change the words and characters?+

Yes. Use Options on the native screensaver, or a terminal configuration file. Words accept commas, line breaks, or a TOML array. They appear vertically in order, then repeat. Leave the words blank to turn them off.

What is ready to download?+

The Mac prerelease is available now: signed, Apple notarized, and stapled. Download the universal package for Apple Silicon and Intel. Physical Intel hardware and longer sessions still need broader testing. Windows and terminal source builds are available now; the latest Windows phrase and dense-theme additions still need manual testing.
